## Narrator script (one ElevenLabs take, the premade "George" voice, `eleven_multilingual_v2`)
1. This is Hast Rekha. Indian palm reading, told as culture and story, not as prediction.
2. Your real palm is the canvas. The headset tracks your hand, and the lines follow your skin.
3. Touch a mount, and its planet's world opens around you.
4. Later, touch any line you chose. Its world opens, and a fist folds it back.
5. It ends with your Rekha card. No camera is used. Only your hand joints, and nothing leaves the headset.
6. Recorded in one take on a Quest 3. The voices and the music are AI generated.
