Hast Rekha

Panditji sits across your table. Hold up your real palm: glowing lines appear on your hand, the seven mounts light up, and you touch each one to hear what the tradition says. Pick the line shapes you see on your own palm, and leave with a Rekha card.

Culture, not prediction. Hast Rekha (part of Samudrika Shastra) is presented as a heritage tradition and for fun. Readings are "what the tradition says", drawn from Cheiro (1916), Benham (1900), the Brihat Samhita and modern Indian guides. Teachers disagree, and nothing here is a forecast or advice.

Privacy. No camera is used. The app reads only the positions of your hand joints, on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Your past cards are stored in this browser only.
