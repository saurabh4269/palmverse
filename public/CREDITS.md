## Palm regions and line shapes
`src/data/palm-regions.json` (mount polygons and line corridors in palm coordinates) and the candidate line shapes in `src/core/line-shapes.ts` were made by the team.
- The mount polygons follow Cheiro's and Benham's anatomical locations.
- The heart, head and life centrelines are mean shapes computed by our offline pipeline (`scripts/palm/`, `research/palm-autolabel/`).
- That pipeline ran [palm-line-reader](https://github.com/samuelwbarber/palm-line-reader) (MIT) on 399 palms from the **11k Hands** dataset (Afifi, 2019, academic use only).
- The landmark means behind the frame calibration also come from 11k Hands statistics.
- No images or per-image data from 11k Hands ship with the app. Only aggregate averages, as coordinates, are included. ⚠ Check that shipping aggregate statistics derived from an academic-use-only dataset is acceptable.

## Baked lighting (made by the team)

## Sound and voice
- **All sound effects are synthesised at runtime** with the Web Audio API in `src/render/audio.ts`: the bell, tick, press, swell, mount tones and drone. No audio files are used.
- **Panditji's voice:** pre-rendered clips in `public/voice/<lang>/`, rendered by `tools/voice/render_voice.py`. The voice is an original synthetic voice created with **ElevenLabs Voice Design** from a text description ("an elderly Indian scholar…"); no real person's voice was cloned. Each clip then goes through `tools/voice/divine.sh` with our own synthetic temple impulse response and tanpura drone (`tools/voice/gen_assets.py`, from the design pack's `research/hast-rekha-audio`; generated with numpy, no third-party audio). Lines without a clip use the browser's built-in `speechSynthesis`.
- **ElevenLabs terms:** output may be used commercially, and without attribution, on a paid plan (Starter or above, per [elevenlabs.io/pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing) and the [Terms of Use](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use)). The free plan requires attribution and is non-commercial. **Status:** all of Panditji's lines are rendered in English and Hindi (18 Nov 2026). The opening voice, **Aakashvani**, is a second original voice made with ElevenLabs Voice Design from its own text description (`tools/voice/voice-divine.json`); it goes through a lighter chain with no drone. Any line without a clip falls back to `speechSynthesis`.
- **Hindi text:** translated from the English script by Claude (AI) for this app. A native-speaker review is pending.

## Music
- **Background music:** 14 looping instrumental beds in `public/audio/music/` (`opening`, the nine grahas `surya chandra mangal budh guru shukra shani rahu ketu`, and the four line worlds `life head heart fate`), listed in `manifest.json` with the prompt used for each track. They were generated with the **ElevenLabs Music API** (`POST /v1/music`, `force_instrumental: true`) from original text prompts in the style of Indian classical and devotional ambient music (tanpura, bansuri, santoor, sitar, sarangi, swarmandal, singing bowls, soft tabla). No existing recording, artist or song was referenced.
- **Post-processing:** done by `tools/music/make_music.py` with ffmpeg. It trims the model's own fade-in and fade-out, crossfades the last 4 s into the head (equal power) so the file loops seamlessly, adds a high-pass at 70 Hz, cuts −4 dB around 2 kHz and applies a high shelf to leave room for the voice, applies a linear gain to −26 LUFS, and encodes the result as MP3 at 96 kbps.
- **Licence:** ElevenLabs output was generated under this project's paid ElevenLabs subscription, which allows commercial use without attribution (the same terms as Panditji's voice above).
